## Where the argument was too narrow

- **Homepage:** “remember, reason, and fail” named activities but stopped before their feedback into the world. The flagship introduction treated memory and control as two topics; “larger working set” offered breadth without a reason for the projects to belong together. The operating method already contained “I debug vertically,” but buried it inside a paragraph.
- **Canonical data and metadata:** the homepage, Chinese homepage, default description, and OG image alternative text used separate summaries. The social image was a static binary with no generator in the repository. Person metadata reduced the direction to a list headed by safety.
- **StrangeLoop:** the existing seven layers were the right architecture. The diagram had no evidence paths, and the full-argument link pointed to a nonexistent anchor. Its institution/time layers named the feedback but did not distinguish the empirical work from the institutional hypothesis.
- **OperatingLoop:** rejection and durable lineage were already strong. Independence needed a sharper meaning than simply a separate gate.
- **VerificationPipeline:** “Externalize truth” treated the evaluator as an authority beyond criticism. Multiple planners were described as independent without demonstrating independent evidence or errors.
- **Research and system pages:** scoped digests and provenance were the strongest material. They needed a route into the cumulative argument, not expanded empirical claims. Recall Debt concerns retrieval; Factor-UT concerns a tested monitoring boundary. Neither establishes a theory of civilization.
- **Notes:** rich discussion of harnesses, institutions, memory, orientation, and feedback already existed. The index foregrounded two specialist August essays while the general framework lacked a clear entry point. Existing unusual notebook language is retained. An audit of the prose is not an independent validation of every older note's external claims.
- **Chinese homepage:** predominantly a translated research/experience inventory. It lacked the deeper notebook entry points and the distinction between measured results and institutional extrapolation.
- **CV and hidden-CoT digest:** factual/reference surfaces; preserve their scope and publication boundaries. Their shared layout receives the synchronized identity and framework navigation.

## Editorial decisions implemented

The first screen frames intelligence as part of the world it changes. The lead traces representation/memory through agents, verifiers, institutions, and feedback; the next sentence asks how that loop can remain inspectable and correctable. “I debug vertically” names the method. Existing sections and components carry the argument.

“Inside the loop” is the one new framework essay, reachable from the hero, notebook, reading navigation, and research digests. It connects Terminal Bench evaluation-integrity questions, memory lineage, decomposition, construct validity, co-adaptation, pluralism, and distributed corrigibility. It marks research findings, operational motivation, a developer's documented deployment report, working hypotheses, and philosophical extrapolation separately. It does not add a publication record for construct-validity work or invent benchmark results.

The essay's AlphaEvolve paragraph follows Google DeepMind's May 14, 2025 primary report, inspected during this pass: https://deepmind.google/blog/alphaevolve-a-gemini-powered-coding-agent-for-designing-advanced-algorithms/. It is attributed as a developer report, not an independent reproduction. No claim about a company's present strategy or inevitable social trajectory is inferred from it.

The seven-layer diagram keeps its structure and interaction, adding layer-specific links to research, system notes, and explicitly labeled hypotheses. The Chinese page now carries the same causal sequence, research connections, and limits on the institutional inference.

`sitePositioning` is the canonical bilingual source for hero language, summary, central question, rotating notes, and Person metadata. A build-generated PNG uses its English hero text for social previews. Article metadata remains specific to each article.
